# ChatGPT Prompt Pack — Visual Directions & Village Map

**How to use (about 10 minutes):**
1. Open ChatGPT and start a **new chat for each prompt** so earlier images don't bleed into the next one.
2. Copy one whole prompt box below (style paragraph included) and paste it in.
3. If ChatGPT offers a size, choose **landscape (wide)**.
4. Save the image you like best and name it as shown (for example `direction_A.png`).
5. Upload the images to me in our Claude chat. I'll check them against the village layout and record them in the asset list.

**Order:** do prompts A–E first. Pick your favorite direction, then do prompt 6 (the map) using that direction's name.

**For the map (prompt 6), also attach `layout_blockout.png`** (the colored layout diagram I sent earlier) so ChatGPT follows the real layout.

**What to look for:** consistent top-down three-quarter angle, light from the upper left, no words or letters in the picture, nothing that looks like an office, and residents' homes that feel distinct.

These are mood images to choose a look. Final game pieces (tiles, sprites) will be redrawn to the 16-pixel grid from whichever look you pick.
