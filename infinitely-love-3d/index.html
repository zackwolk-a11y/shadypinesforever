FAQs

What is The Infinite Care Circle Membership?

It is our flexible, month-to-month plan designed to make continuous self-care a priority with Infinitely Love Healing Arts. For a fixed monthly fee, you secure one session credit per month and unlock a significant discount across all our services.

How much is the membership?

The monthly fee is $105.

What is the biggest member benefit?

Your top benefits are Priority Booking (allowing you to book appointments up to 3 months in advance) and a 15% Discount applied to every single service we offer, including longer massages, private yoga, meditation, and fitness.

Using Your Credits

What is included in my monthly fee?

Your $105 fee secures you one 60-Minute Session Credit.

What if I want a 90-minute massage instead?

Your $105 monthly fee acts as a credit that can be applied to any service. You simply pay the discounted difference at the time of your appointment. Example: For a 90-minute massage, the final out-of-pocket cost is only $43.75 after applying your 15% discount and your $105 monthly credit.

Does my discount apply to other services like yoga?

Yes. The 15% Member Discount applies to all of your services: private yoga, fusion bellydance, guided meditation, and functional fitness training sessions.

Policies and Expiration

What if I don’t use my credit one month? Does it roll over?

Yes. All unused monthly credits roll over and accumulate in your account as long as your membership is active.

How long do I have to use my rolled-over credits?

All accumulated credits expire one year (12 months) from the original date of purchase. We recommend booking regularly to utilize your benefits.

Can I cancel my membership anytime?

Yes. The membership is month-to-month. You can cancel or freeze anytime without penalty by simply providing written notice before your next scheduled billing date.

What happens to my credits if I cancel?

Upon cancellation, any remaining unexpired credits must be redeemed within 90 days.
